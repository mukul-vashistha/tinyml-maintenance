# My TinyML lab record

This private record lives at `.tinyml-lab/student-log.md`.

You can write each entry yourself, or the AI coach can draft and save it from your conversation. When AI writes, review the saved text. Remove anything you did not say, inspect, run, or understand.

## Phase 00 starter format

Use a simple record while you are learning the project story:

```text
Phase: 00

What I think the project is trying to do:

Why the prediction is useful:

Actions it may support:

Files or results we inspected:

What AI explained:

What I can explain now:

Drafting note:
Written by me / Drafted by AI from my answers and reviewed by me
```

It is acceptable to have no rejected AI suggestion in Phase 00.

## Guided-phase format

Use this from Phase 01 onward:

```text
Phase:

Question we were answering:

My prediction or initial explanation:

What AI explained or proposed:

Evidence we inspected or ran:

What I accepted:

What I revised, rejected, or still question:

What the result means:

What I understand now:

Drafting note:
Written by me / Drafted by AI from my answers and reviewed by me
```

Short answers are fine. The record must be honest enough that you can explain it later.

## Later-phase handoff

For Phases 07 and 08, also include:

```text
Operating decision:
Remaining risk:
Evidence needed next:
```
