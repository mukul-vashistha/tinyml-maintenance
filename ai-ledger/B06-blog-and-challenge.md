# B06 — Blog and extension challenge

AI proposed a conventional technical article organized by algorithms. Review rejected that structure because it separated ML from the development process.

The accepted article pairs every ML question with a context packet, blueprint prompt, bounded patch, review stack, command, artifact, and repository link. Numeric claims are rendered from experiment JSON. Full chat transcripts are omitted; decision-carrying excerpts and complete ledger records remain in the repository.

The challenge begins from the working repository and evaluates AI usage, application, and understanding of limits alongside ML judgment and shipping quality.
