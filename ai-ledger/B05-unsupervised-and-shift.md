# B05 — Unsupervised views and shift

AI proposed multiple clustering and visualization methods. The engineer required each to state its assumption and prohibited cluster plots from being treated as evidence of deployable categories.

PCA is used as a two-dimensional view. K-means, DBSCAN, and Gaussian mixtures are compared against the generator's hidden regime only for validation. Future-time and unseen-type splits show where ordinary evaluation stops applying.
